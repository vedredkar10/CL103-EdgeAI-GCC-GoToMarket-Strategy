# MoSCoW Prioritisation — Strategic Requirements
**Project:** GCC Edge AI Go-To-Market Strategy
**Client:** Dubai-based Edge AI hardware venture (anonymised)
**BA:** Ved Redkar | Visionize Consultancy LLP
**Version:** 1.0

---

## Context
MoSCoW prioritisation was applied to the full set of strategic
requirements extracted during stakeholder elicitation. Requirements
were prioritised against the core business objective:

> **Achieve a defensible market foothold within 18 months,
> starting from zero brand equity and zero market credibility.**

The prioritisation framework reflects sequencing constraints — some
Could Have requirements become Must Have in later phases once earlier
gates are passed.

---

## Must Have

| Req ID | Requirement | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| M-001 | Define clear deployment SKUs — Traffic, Surveillance, Smart Parking — with workload-specific validation | Without SKU clarity, every ISV conversation starts from scratch. SKU definition is the foundation of all subsequent engagement. |
| M-002 | Build internal benchmarking capability before any external engagement | Benchmark data is the entry ticket to ISV conversations. Without it, technical validation engagements cannot be proposed credibly. |
| M-003 | Master Axelera SDK deeply — internal technical competency | Cannot support ISV integration or validate workloads without deep SDK knowledge. This is Phase 1 internal work that cannot be skipped. |
| M-004 | Narrow initial use cases to 2–3 fixed-function workloads | ANPR, vehicle detection, people counting are fixed-function workloads that do not require GPU flexibility. This is where efficiency advantage is most defensible. |
| M-005 | Achieve 2 signed technical validation engagements by month 6 | Without ISV validation proof, no SI conversation is credible. Two signed engagements is the minimum viable proof set. |
| M-006 | Extended temperature certification (50°C sustained) | Non-negotiable qualification criterion for GCC outdoor infrastructure procurement. Cannot proceed to SI engagement without it. |
| M-007 | IP54+ certification — validated not claimed | Same as above. Self-claimed certification is a disqualifying red flag in enterprise procurement. |

---

## Should Have

| Req ID | Requirement | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| S-001 | Co-author at least one technical whitepaper with ISV partner by month 9 | Converts validation engagement into publishable proof. Differentiates from hardware vendors who only claim performance. |
| S-002 | Structured SI collaboration — MOU or RFP participation by month 12 | Opens the pathway to 50–150 node deployments. SI relationships take time to build — starting month 9 conversations gives time to formalise by month 12. |
| S-003 | Lifecycle warranty programme defined and published before Phase 4 | Required for enterprise procurement. Not a Phase 1 priority but must be in place before SI conversations begin. |
| S-004 | Thermal stress test documentation available to ISV/SI partners | Supports technical due diligence. ISVs will request this during validation engagements. |
| S-005 | Reference architecture documentation per deployment SKU | Reduces ISV integration time and demonstrates deployment intelligence beyond hardware specification. |

---

## Could Have

| Req ID | Requirement | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| C-001 | GITEX attendance with validated deployment story by month 18 | High-value if achieved — positions the client as a validated infrastructure solution at the region's most important technology event. Contingent on Phase 5 deployment being completed. |
| C-002 | Two public production case studies published by month 18 | Provides credibility foundation for press releases and subsequent enterprise conversations. Depends on Phase 3 deployment completion. |
| C-003 | TCO modelling tool for SI prospects | Accelerates SI commercial conversations. Useful but the TCO case can initially be made manually in presentations. |

---

## Won't Have (this phase)

| Req ID | Requirement | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| W-001 | Broad marketing campaign or press releases before deployment proof | Press without validation is noise. Marketing spend before proof exists damages credibility rather than building it. |
| W-002 | Multi-vertical simultaneous ISV engagement | Spreading engagement across retail, logistics, surveillance, and robotics simultaneously dilutes focus and resources. Win one vertical deeply first. |
| W-003 | Hardware redesign per vertical | Manufacturing complexity, inventory chaos, and support burden would undermine the efficiency and scalability advantages. Hardware stays constant — deployment layer varies. |
| W-004 | Direct end-customer sales before SI relationship | Infrastructure procurement at scale goes through SIs. Attempting direct sales before SI relationships exist bypasses the procurement pathway and wastes credibility. |

## Prioritisation Summary

| Priority | Count | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Must Have | 7 | Internal readiness + first ISV validation |
| Should Have | 5 | Proof generation + SI pathway opening |
| Could Have | 3 | Scale and market presence |
| Won't Have | 4 | Premature activities that damage credibility |
